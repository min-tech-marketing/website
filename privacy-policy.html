Overview

Min-Tech Marketing Inc. and Min-Tech Environmental Inc. (together, "Min-Tech", "we", "us") are members of the group of companies comprising CLN Global Inc. and its direct and indirect subsidiaries and affiliates (collectively, the "CLN Companies"). Min-Tech operates this website (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy (this "Policy") explains how we collect, use, and disclose information on the Website. Min-Tech is responsible for personal information collected through the Website, except where another member of the CLN Companies is identified as responsible for a particular processing activity.

This Policy

This Policy demonstrates our commitment to protecting your privacy and is incorporated into our Terms of Use. We may use your personal information to provide and improve this Website and our services. Our collection, use and disclosure of personal information is governed by this Policy and applicable law, including any consent requirements that apply to particular processing activities.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

This Website may collect personal information that identifies you, such as your name, company, email address and phone number. Collection of personal information through this Website will not be excessive, and we will not ask for information such as social insurance numbers, banking details or date of birth.

On a voluntary basis, you may provide personal information through the contact form on this Website to ask about our services, request a quote, or apply for a role. We do not sell personal information collected through the Website. We may disclose personal information as described in this Policy, with your consent where required, or as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

Cookies, Analytics and Tracking Technologies

We and our third-party service providers may use cookies, pixels, tags, scripts and similar technologies (collectively, "Cookies") to operate the Website, understand how visitors use and interact with it, measure its performance and improve our services.

In particular, we may use Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console (Google) and Microsoft Clarity. Depending on the tool and your interaction with the Website, these third parties may collect or receive information such as your IP address, device and browser information, approximate location, pages viewed, referring and exit pages, time spent on the Website, navigation paths, clicks, scrolling and other interactions, and identifiers stored in or derived from Cookies.

We use non-essential Cookies only where permitted by applicable law and, where consent is required, only after obtaining your consent. You may accept, reject or manage non-essential Cookies through the cookie banner shown on your first visit. Rejecting non-essential Cookies will not prevent you from accessing the Website.

You may withdraw or change your consent at any time through the , including the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of every page. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before it was withdrawn.

Cookie Categories and Choices

When you first visit the Website, we ask which categories of non-essential Cookies you allow. Non-essential Cookies are off until you opt in:

  • Essential: required for the Website to operate and to remember your cookie choices. Always on.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity, which help us understand which pages are useful and how visitors navigate the Website. Clarity may record pseudonymized interactions with Website pages, such as clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, to produce session replays and heatmaps.

Microsoft Clarity is not loaded unless you allow Analytics Cookies. Google Analytics 4 is loaded in Google Consent Mode: unless you allow Analytics Cookies, it does not set or read Cookies, but it may send Google limited, cookieless signals (such as the fact that a page was viewed, along with basic browser and device information) that Google may use to produce aggregated and modelled statistics.

We store your cookie choices in your browser’s local storage (under the name mt-cookie-consent) for 12 months, after which we will ask you again. If you withdraw consent, the page reloads so that the change takes effect; Cookies already set may remain in your browser until they expire or you delete them.

How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information collected through the Website to: (a) operate, maintain, secure and improve the Website; (b) respond to inquiries and communications submitted to us; (c) understand and analyze how visitors access, navigate and interact with the Website; (d) measure Website traffic, usage and performance; (e) manage our customer, business and prospective business relationships; (f) consider applications for employment; (g) detect, investigate and prevent security incidents, fraud, misuse or other unlawful activity; (h) comply with applicable legal, regulatory and reporting obligations; and (i) establish, exercise or defend legal rights and claims.

Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy applicable legal, accounting, reporting and compliance requirements, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. When personal information is no longer required, we will delete, destroy or anonymize it in accordance with applicable law.

Security

We treat your personal information as confidential and make reasonable efforts to protect it. However, as data transmission over the Internet is not completely secure, we cannot guarantee that such information will not be lost, used unlawfully or modified.

External Links

This Website may contain links to external websites. This Policy does not extend to the data collection practices of third parties, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. We recommend reading their privacy policies before submitting personal information to them.

These providers may include Google, HubSpot and Microsoft.

Personal information may therefore be processed or stored outside the province or country in which you reside, and may be accessible to courts, law enforcement or governmental authorities in those jurisdictions in accordance with applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may change this Policy from time to time. Any changes will comply with applicable privacy laws and will be posted on this page of the Website.

Children's Privacy

Depending on where you reside, you may have rights in relation to your personal information, including the right to request access to or correction of it, request its deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
