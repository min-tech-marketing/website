Our clientele commitment

Two relationships, one zero-waste loop

Client relationship 1 — feedstock providers

Companies attempting to part with industrial scrap using environmentally friendly methods. Our greatest strength is these relationships, particularly with one manufacturer which provides an LDPE/fibre mix.

By patiently developing that relationship over several years, Min-Tech signed an agreement in 2002 with a Fortune 500 company in the consumer goods sector to take all the scrap from its facility. Since 2002 this has provided Min-Tech with a consistent and predictable supply of 100% clean, high quality, post-industrial grade plastic scrap at a fixed price well below rates available on the secondary scrap market.

Min-Tech research indicates this feedstock relationship is truly rare — there are only a handful of manufacturing facilities in North America large enough to provide the volume of reclaimed post-industrial grade HDPE/LDPE scrap required to maintain a plastic plywood manufacturing operation of any scale.

Client relationship 2 — buyers

Buyers of industrial scrap, or of goods manufactured by Min-Tech from that scrap. All of our type 2 clients are located around the world, specializing in the recycling industry for over 30 years.

With that reach we have launched dozens of products into the market, creating both customer and supplier loyalty. Over the years we have built a very loyal client base of companies who need to part with industrial scrap.

We provide zero-waste solutions, which has created significant barriers to entry for competitors looking to displace us.
