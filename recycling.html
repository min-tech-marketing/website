What we do

More than just recycling.

Min-Tech recycling does more than just recycle, burn or refurbish plastics. We give plastic a whole new meaning and a whole new purpose.

We use clean, unprocessed industrial scrap and plastics from leading consumer goods manufacturers. We handle all different types of plastics — ranging from old DVDs and car parts to water bottles.

Our goal

A greener planet, one second life at a time.

By finding a second life for discarded industrial scrap, we are reducing our carbon footprint and giving all our plastics a new life.

If we all put our part in finding new lives for our used or unused plastics, our planet will become greener with each given day.

New program

The Min-Tech Buy Back Program

Your clean industrial plastic scrap is worth more than landfill tipping fees. Through our new Buy Back Program, Min-Tech pays you for qualifying post-industrial scrap — then gives it a second life in our Canadian-made spill control products.

It is the same zero-waste service our feedstock partners have relied on for over twenty years, now with a cheque attached.

Get a buy back quote (705) 632-0111
  1. 1Send us a description and photo of your scrap stream — type, volume and how often it is generated.
  2. 2We assess the material and come back with a per-pound buy back price.
  3. 3We arrange pickup from your facility on a schedule that suits your production.
  4. 4You get paid, and your scrap stays out of landfill.
Materials

What we accept

Clean, unprocessed industrial and post-industrial scrap plastic from leading consumer goods manufacturers.

HDPE & LDPE

Post-industrial grade high- and low-density polyethylene scrap, including LDPE/fibre mixes, in consistent high-volume streams.

Rigid plastics

Discs, casings and moulded components — from old DVDs to automotive parts — recovered before they reach the waste stream.

Containers & packaging

Bottles, containers and packaging scrap baled and reclaimed for reprocessing into new products.
