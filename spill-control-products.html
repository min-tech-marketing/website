Manufacturing

Spill Control Products

High quality absorbent products manufactured in Canada with recycled content to handle your leak and spill needs.

Made in Canada

Built from recycled plastic and cellulose.

Min-Tech is committed to providing high quality absorbent products manufactured in Canada with recycled content to handle leak and spill needs. Min-Tech's spill control products quickly address spills on water or land.

Our products are produced from recycled plastic and cellulose materials to absorb most types of spills such as liquids, chemicals, oils and oil-based fluids.

Min-Tech's heavy weight mats absorb acids, chemicals and unknown liquids quickly and won't fall apart in an emergency. Our high-capacity booms can soak up gallons of oils or water, and our commercial grade loose absorbent works quickly to absorb spills and leaks on contact — drying quickly for an easy cleanup.

Applications

Where our spill control products go to work

On open water

Min-Tech's mats float even when completely saturated — ideal for spills on lakes, rivers and ocean shorelines.

Around equipment

Ideal for use around heavy machinery, leaky pipes and messy traffic areas where drips and spills are routine.

Hard-to-reach places

Designed to fit under machines and into confined spaces such as sumps and catch basins.

Min-Tech manufactures hundreds of different spill control products for leading wholesalers — including pads, rolls, socks, booms, drum tops and loose products.
