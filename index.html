Spill control products

Made in Canada, from recycled content.

Our absorbents quickly address spills on water or land — liquids, chemicals, oils and oil-based fluids. Heavy weight mats absorb acids and unknown liquids and won't fall apart in an emergency; high-capacity booms soak up gallons of oil or water; and our commercial grade loose absorbent dries quickly for an easy cleanup.

Our Buy Back Program

Your scrap is worth more than tipping fees.

Through the Min-Tech Buy Back Program, we pay you for qualifying clean post-industrial plastic scrap — then give it a second life in our Canadian-made spill control products.

It is the same zero-waste service our feedstock partners have relied on for over twenty years, now with a cheque attached.

Get a buy back quote (705) 632-0111
  1. 01

    Tell us about your scrap

    Send a description and photo of your stream — type, volume and how often it is generated.

  2. 02

    We assess and price it

    We review the material and come back with a per-pound buy back price.

  3. 03

    We arrange pickup

    Collection from your facility on a schedule that suits your production.

  4. 04

    You get paid

    Your scrap stays out of landfill, and you get a cheque instead of a disposal bill.
