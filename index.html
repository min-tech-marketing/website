What our innovation team can do for you

Advanced recycling

We recycle unprocessed, clean industrial and post-industrial scrap plastic. We've designed recovery pathways for materials as far-ranging as from car parts to water bottles.

Product destruction

Environmentally-friendly destruction of off-spec, obsolete and surplus plastic products. Any materials we can recover are reprocessed rather than burned or sent to landfill.

Zero-waste solutions

Custom recycled product manufacturing

Once we've recovered your material, we can help customize its new use, from cradle to cradle. Our manufacturing and wholesale partners are using their own recovered materials to drive revenue and impact in adjacent product streams.

Closed-loop recovery

Customer case study: spill control products

Every manufacturer runs on inputs, and every production line leaves something behind. One major consumer products manufacturer wanted to upcycle rejected personal care materials by exploring broader recycling technologies in partnership with Min-Tech.

The client's global asset recovery team targeted factory-level waste reduction, though scaling beyond manufacturing scrap to post-consumer mixed plastic waste remained a distinct industry challenge. These products are up to 90% plastic, making consumer-level recycling complex and expensive because the components are difficult to separate.

Min-Tech's materials scientists designed a pathway that does more than divert this material. We paired secure, traceable recovery with processing for the manufacturer's own range of spill control products, turning yesterday's offcuts into tomorrow's inventory.

The result is one partner and one closed loop: lower logistics and landfill costs, fully traceable diversion, and new revenue unlocked by putting the original inputs back to work.
