Our services

Get leading diversion pathway design, from a recycling industry leader.

Our materials scientists have pioneered durable materials solutions for post-industrial plastic scrap. Our full-service offering is built on deep expertise from long-term loyal supplier and customer relationships with leading consumer packaged goods manufacturers. We take clean industrial scrap out of your waste streams and find new ways to put it back to work.

What our innovation team can do for you

Advanced recycling

We recycle unprocessed, clean industrial and post-industrial scrap plastic. We've designed recovery pathways for materials as far-ranging as from car parts to water bottles.

Product destruction

Environmentally-friendly destruction of off-spec, obsolete and surplus plastic products. Any materials we can recover are reprocessed rather than burned or sent to landfill.

Zero-waste solutions

For companies with public ESG commitments, we build programs that make progress, on a pickup and volume schedule that suits your production needs.

Custom recycled product manufacturing

Once we've recovered your material, we can help customize its new use, from cradle to cradle. Our manufacturing and wholesale partners are using their own recovered materials to drive revenue and impact in adjacent product streams.

Closed-loop recovery

Customer case study: spill control products

Every manufacturer runs on inputs, and every production line leaves something behind. For one major consumer products manufacturer in Canada, that was a steady stream of high-quality absorbent trim from the cutting-room floor: post-industrial material far too valuable to bury in landfill.

Min-Tech's materials scientists designed a pathway that does more than divert it. We paired secure, traceable recovery with processing for the manufacturer's own range of spill control products, turning yesterday's offcuts into tomorrow's inventory.

The result is one partner and one closed loop: lower logistics and landfill costs, fully traceable diversion, and new revenue unlocked by putting the original inputs back to work.

Our Buy Back Program

Your scrap is worth more than tipping fees.

Through the Min-Tech Buy Back Program, we pay you for qualifying clean post-industrial plastic scrap — then give it a second life in our Canadian-made spill control products.

It is the same zero-waste service our feedstock partners have relied on for over twenty years, now with a cheque attached.
