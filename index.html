About Min-Tech

We make our products from recycled post-commercial content.

Min-Tech has been a pioneer in environmentally friendly industrial plastic scrap recycling since 1998. Our 42,000 square foot facility sits on fifteen acres in the Tanner Industrial Park in Campbellford, Ontario.

With a unique full-service offering, Min-Tech is a leader in helping manufacturers divert waste away from landfill by finding a second life for discarded industrial scrap.

What we do

Two sides of the same commitment

We take clean industrial scrap out of the waste stream — and put it back to work as high-performance absorbent products.

Recycling

Min-Tech recycles clean, unprocessed industrial and post-industrial scrap plastic from leading consumer goods manufacturers — helping them reduce their carbon footprint by diverting tonnes of plastic from landfills.

Manufacturing

Min-Tech manufactures spill control products made from clean, unprocessed industrial plastic scrap. Our products are sold through wholesalers and distributors throughout North America and Asia.

Our mission
Canadian owned & operated

Three and a half decades in the scrap sector.

Min-Tech is 100% Canadian owned and operated by Dale McLellan, who brings 35 years of experience in the scrap sector. He spent 10 years operating a plastic pelletizing manufacturing facility in Quebec.

From 1991 to 2000, Min-Tech spent considerable time in China, consulting with partners to modernize five plastic manufacturing facilities by outfitting them with U.S. equipment and more efficient processes — improving end-product quality and increasing throughput.

Min-Tech also supplied Trex, a major plastic/lumber manufacturer, with millions of pounds of reclaimed plastic scrap over a two-year period.
